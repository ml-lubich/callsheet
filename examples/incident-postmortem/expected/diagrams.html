Three figures, read in order. The first lays the whole incident on its own clock, from the config change to the moment the backlog hit zero, and marks which points on that clock were a person acting versus the system reacting on its own. The second opens the middle of that clock into a causal chain, from the change to the moment a customer saw a duplicate email. The third steps back and shows how the night actually split up, and gives the two decision points — the wrong fix and the right one — the same colors they carry in the first two figures.

Two of those eight points are decisions a person made, twelve minutes apart on this clock; the timeline cannot show why only one of them made things worse — that needs the mechanism.

That chain accounts for the failure; it says nothing about the time lost on either side of it, which is what the last figure is for.
